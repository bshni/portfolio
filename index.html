The challenge

The Sample Superstore dataset arrived with missing values, duplicate rows, inconsistent formatting, and no engineered features — just raw transactions with no clear story yet.

What I built

A reusable, chainable object-oriented preprocessing pipeline with proper exception handling, plus engineered features: Profit Margin, Shipping Duration, Sales Performance Category, Discount Bucket, and calendar-based fields. Correlation, statistical significance, and trend analysis followed using scipy.stats.

The result

Eleven Matplotlib and Seaborn visualizations, an assembled dashboard, automated KPI summaries, and a final analytical report identifying the clearest drivers of profitability and shipping efficiency.
